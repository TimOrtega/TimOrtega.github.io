A memorial for Bella

Using the tools I’m learning to make something that matters to me.

Personal projectLaser-cut MDFCAD to fabrication
The story

I made this bone-shaped memorial for my dog Bella. Designing and making it gave me a way to turn something deeply personal into a physical keepsake, displayed beside photographs of her.

Project focus
  • Bone-shaped CAD profile
  • Laser-cut MDF
  • Personalized lettering
Explore 3D models ↓
01 / A personal purpose

Made to remember.

This project began with a purpose rather than a mechanism: I wanted to make a memorial for Bella. The bone-shaped outline and personalized lettering make the piece specific to her and to the memories it represents.

It is a reminder that design can be meaningful on a small scale. The care put into a shape, its proportions, and how it is displayed matters just as much as the tools used to produce it.

02 / CAD to laser cutter

A digital outline.
A lasting keepsake.

I translated the bone-shaped design into laser-cut MDF. The work connected the CAD profile, the placement of lettering, and the physical material, creating a piece that sits alongside photographs of Bella.

The model below documents the geometry. The photograph shows the finished memorial in its intended setting.
